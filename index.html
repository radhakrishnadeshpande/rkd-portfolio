Documents are where I started, but the tools I use (vision models, representation learning, careful evaluation on noisy data) apply well beyond them. I'm looking for interdisciplinary research where machine learning meets the physical and life sciences.

Robust vision models for scans where labels are scarce and noise is real, which is the same problem as my label-free OCR work.
